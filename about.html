Meet Attia Altaf

Integrative Psychotherapist

I am Attia Altaf, an Integrative Psychotherapist. I have more than 8 years' experience helping individuals navigate the complexities of mental health in Pakistan.

My journey began when I noticed the huge gap in accessible mental health care in our country. Social stigma, traffic, and high costs often prevent people from seeking help.

"Healing with Attia" was born out of a desire to create a safe, non-judgmental digital space where you can share your burden without fear. Whether it's societal pressure, academic stress, or marital issues, I am here to listen.

My approach is integrative — I draw on several evidence-based methods, including CBT, DBT and trauma-informed care, and tailor them to each person rather than applying a single formula. Trained under the UK’s CPCAB framework, I combine international clinical standards with a deep understanding of Pakistani family, cultural, and religious realities. I work with clients across Islamabad, Lahore, Karachi, and the wider Pakistani diaspora, in Urdu, English, and Punjabi.
