What is Therapy, Really?

At its simplest, therapy is a series of private conversations that help you feel and function better. Think of it not as a hospital for the "sick," but as a gym for your mind — a confidential space where we make sense of difficult thoughts and emotions and build practical tools for a lighter, more resilient life. In each session, you talk, I listen and ask questions, and together we find a way forward.

In our culture, we are often told to "just pray" or "be strong." Therapy doesn't replace faith or resilience — it supports them. It simply gives you a judgment-free space to say the things you can't say to anyone else.

Therapy is

  • Confidential & judgment-free
  • Practical, for everyday struggles
  • A partnership — you stay in control
  • A sign of strength, not weakness

Therapy isn't

  • Only for "serious" mental illness
  • A replacement for faith
  • Being told what to do
  • Something that lasts forever

Is Therapy Right For Me?

  • Do you feel like you're carrying the weight of the world, but have to keep smiling?
  • Is it hard to switch off your brain at night due to worry?
  • Do you feel lonely, even when surrounded by family or friends?
  • Are you tired of repeating the same painful patterns in relationships?
  • Do you worry that seeking help makes you "ungrateful" or "weak"?

If you said yes to even one of these, that's reason enough to reach out.
