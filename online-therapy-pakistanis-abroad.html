Talking to Family Back Home

Telling parents in Pakistan that you're seeing a therapist can feel harder than the therapy itself, especially on a video call across a time difference. Some things that help: call at a calm time in their day, describe how you've been rather than naming a diagnosis, and mention that the counsellor is Pakistani and works in Urdu.

There is a short script in Urdu and English in how to talk to your parents about therapy.

If You Need Help Right Now

Online therapy is not an emergency service. If you are thinking of harming yourself or are in danger, use the emergency services where you live:

  • Canada: 911 for emergencies; call or text 988 for the Suicide Crisis Helpline.
  • US: 911 for emergencies; call or text 988, the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.
  • Australia: 000 for emergencies; Lifeline on 13 11 14.
  • Germany, the Netherlands, Finland and the rest of the EU: 112.
  • Norway: 113 for medical emergencies, 112 for police (local guidance).
  • Qatar: 999.
  • UK: 999, or NHS 111 (mental health option); Samaritans on 116 123.

Anywhere else, call your local emergency number or go to the nearest hospital emergency department.
