Marriage Counseling in Lahore — Always One-to-One

On Zoom, in Punjabi, Urdu or English: whichever language the arguments at home actually happen in.

Looking for marriage counseling in Lahore usually means something at home has been wrong for a while, and talking it over with family hasn't helped. These are the questions Lahore clients ask first.

My husband or wife won't come. Is there any point?

Yes. Attia only ever works one-to-one, so nothing waits for your spouse to agree. You look at your own side: what you say when things heat up, which boundaries you keep, how you repair after a fight. When one person changes how they respond, the pattern between two people often shifts as well.

Will anyone in the family know?

Only if you tell them. Attia shares nothing with relatives or in-laws, nothing is ever recorded, and you won't be seen walking into a clinic in Gulberg or DHA.

Is there counseling between the rishta and the nikah?

Yes, one-to-one pre-marital counseling. Between the rishta being agreed and the nikah, you work through what you expect, from money and living with in-laws to faith and children, and how to raise each one with your future spouse. See the pre-marital guide.

What is the first session like?

Mostly listening. Attia asks what brought you, what home is like day to day and what you have tried so far. You leave with an agreed focus for the next few sessions; nothing about the marriage has to be decided in the first hour.

Does marriage counseling cost more?

No, it is the same flat fee as every session. The fee, and what other Lahore practices charge, is further down this page.

If You Need Help Right Now

Online sessions can't help in an emergency. If you are in danger, or thinking about ending your life, in Lahore:

  • 1122: Punjab Emergency Service (Rescue 1122), which operates in every district of Punjab.
  • 15, then press 7: since January 2026 the Punjab Safe Cities Authority's Police Emergency Helpline 15 connects callers facing mental stress or suicidal thoughts to a psychologist, free of charge (Daily Independent).
  • Punjab Institute of Mental Health (PIMH), Shadman-II, Jail Road: its emergency department sees psychiatric patients round the clock. Phone +92 42 99203776-7.
  • Umang mental health helpline: +92 311 7786264 (listed by GOV.UK).

Or go to the emergency department of the nearest hospital.

From Lahore: Times, Language and Dropped Calls

Sessions are held Monday to Saturday between 4pm and 8pm. From Lahore that means a late-afternoon slot or one after office hours, taken at home, in the car or anywhere you can't be overheard. Plenty of Lahore clients mix Punjabi and Urdu in one sentence; that is fine, and so is English.

If the call drops

Power cuts and patchy internet happen in Lahore, and on Attia's side in Pakistan as well, so the connection can go at either end. If it does, you call back and pick up where you left off.

When to go somewhere else first

Don't start with online sessions if you might hurt yourself or someone else, if you think you need medication, or if home isn't safe for you. Attia can't prescribe; that takes a psychiatrist, who is a medical doctor. In Lahore, the Punjab Institute of Mental Health on Jail Road runs an emergency department round the clock, Rescue 1122 covers every district of Punjab, and Helpline 15 (press 7) now puts you through to a psychologist.

Otherwise, video sessions are real therapy: UCLA Health reports that across more than 60 studies they worked as well as sitting in the same room with a therapist, for most people with anxiety, depression or PTSD; and it advises in-person care for more severe conditions.
